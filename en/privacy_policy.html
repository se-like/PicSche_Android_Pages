Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

PicSche (pronounced "Pikuske"; "this app") respects your privacy and takes the protection of personal information seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

This app accesses the following information:

(1) Camera & Photo Library
Purpose: To select or capture images containing schedule information (e.g., school handouts, event flyers)
Storage: Images are deleted immediately after schedule extraction and are not stored within the app

(2) Calendar
Purpose: To register extracted schedule information to your device calendar
Storage: Schedule information is saved to the device's calendar app and not to our own servers

(3) Text in Images
Purpose: To extract schedule information (title, date, location, etc.) via AI analysis
Storage: Extracted information is temporarily held in memory and deleted after calendar registration

(4) Push Notification Device Identifier
Purpose: To send a push notification when analysis is complete (e.g., "Analysis complete!")
Storage: Stored with a device identifier (FCM token) in a cloud service provided by Google LLC. Tokens are overwritten on update.
Disabling: If you turn off notifications for PicSche in Android Settings → Apps → PicSche → Notifications, we will not register new identifiers. Existing identifiers will be removed from our records when you delete the app.

2. Third-Party Services

This app uses external AI analysis services to accurately extract schedule information from images.

(1) Information Sent

  • Images selected or captured by the user
  • Instructions for image analysis (including text you enter in the "Customization" field)
  • For free-tier usage we may manage usage on our server and send a device identifier (based on Android ID; not personally identifiable); usage count and the relevant month may be stored on our server.
  • We may save usage and purchase-related records to our systems. We retain them for a certain period as needed for fraud prevention and service operation.

(2) Purpose of Transmission
To automatically extract schedule information (title, date, location, notes) via AI

(3) About the Customization Field
Content you enter in the Customization field is sent to the selected AI provider as-is. Please do not enter personal information (name, address, phone number, email, etc.) or confidential information. Input is limited to 500 characters.

(4) AI Services Used
This app uses one of the following for AI analysis (depending on the app version):
・Anthropic Claude (https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)
・OpenAI GPT-4o (https://openai.com/privacy)
・Google Gemini (https://policies.google.com/privacy)
・Google Cloud AI (via our server; https://cloud.google.com/terms/cloud-privacy-notice)
Supported services may change without notice for improved functionality.

(5) Data Handling

  • Images are deleted from the server immediately after analysis
  • Processed in accordance with each AI service provider's privacy policy
  • We configure so that AI providers do not use user data for training

(6) Protection of Personal Information
We strive not to extract personally identifiable information (name, address, phone number, etc.) that may appear in images. We cannot guarantee complete prevention given the nature of image content.

(7) Push Notifications (Google)
We use a cloud service from Google LLC to send push notifications when analysis is complete.
・Information stored: device identifier, analysis completion records
・Google privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
・If you disable notifications, we do not register new identifiers.

(8) Advertising
This app does not display ads.

3. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used only for: extracting schedule information from images, registering extracted schedules to the calendar, improving app functionality and fixing defects.

This app does NOT use information for: user behavior tracking, selling or providing data to third parties (except AI analysis use).

4. Data Retention

  • Image data: Deleted immediately after analysis (retention: seconds)
  • Extracted schedule information: Deleted from the app after calendar registration
  • Registered calendar events: Stored in the device calendar app (until the user deletes them)

This app does not store images or schedule content on servers. We may store or retain: free-tier usage (usage count and month linked to a device identifier); push notification identifiers on Google's service; usage and purchase-related records in our systems for a certain period for fraud prevention and service operation.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except: when required by law, when you have given consent, as described in "Third-Party Services" (AI analysis use) above.

6. Security

We implement the following to protect your information: temporary storage of images only (no permanent storage), encrypted communication, minimization of in-app data, configuration to prohibit use of data for AI training.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: revoke access to camera, photos, calendar, and notifications (via Android Settings), delete the app (all app data will be removed), delete calendar events (via the device calendar app).

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. If a child under 13 uses this app, it should be with parental consent and supervision.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be changed without notice due to legal changes or app updates. Significant changes will be notified within the app.
