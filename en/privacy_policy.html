Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

PicSche ("this app") respects your privacy and takes the protection of personal information seriously. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

This app accesses the following information:

(1) Camera & Photo Library
Purpose: To select or capture images containing schedule information (e.g., school handouts, event flyers) and send them to our server (or the AI analysis service in use) for AI-based schedule extraction
Storage: Images are deleted immediately after schedule extraction and are not stored within the app

(1-b) PDF Files
Purpose: To select PDFs containing schedule information (e.g., school newsletters) and send them to our server for AI-based schedule extraction. PDFs are analyzed as files without converting pages to images on the device
Storage: PDFs are deleted immediately after schedule extraction and are not stored within the app

(2) Calendar
Purpose: To register extracted schedule information to your Android calendar
Storage: Schedule information is saved to the OS default calendar app and not to our own servers

(3) Text in Images
Purpose: To extract schedule information (title, date, location, etc.) via AI analysis
Storage: Extracted information is temporarily held in memory and deleted after calendar registration

(4) Push Notification Device Identifier
Purpose: To send a push notification when analysis is complete
Storage: Stored with a device identifier in a cloud service provided by Google LLC. Tokens are overwritten on update.
Disabling: If you turn off notifications for PicSche in Android settings, we will not register new identifiers. After you uninstall the app, we do not send notifications to that device. We may remove existing identifiers from our records when we find they are invalid (e.g., when a send fails) or as part of routine maintenance; they are not automatically deleted at the moment you uninstall.

2. Third-Party Services

This app uses external AI analysis services to accurately extract schedule information from images or PDFs.

(1) Information Sent

  • Images selected or captured by the user, or PDFs selected by the user
  • Instructions for image/PDF analysis (including text you enter in the "Customization" field)
  • For free-tier usage we may manage usage on our server and send a device identifier; usage count and the relevant month may be stored on our server.
  • We may save usage and purchase-related records to our systems. We retain them for a certain period as needed for fraud prevention and service operation.

(2) Purpose of Transmission
To automatically extract schedule information (title, date, location, notes) via AI

(3) About the Customization Field
Content you enter in the Customization field is sent to the selected AI provider as-is. Please do not enter personal information (name, address, phone number, email, etc.) or confidential information. Input is limited to 500 characters.

(4) AI Services Used
This app uses one of the following AI analysis services (we may unify to Vertex AI via our API; depending on build configuration or app version):
・Anthropic Claude (link)
・OpenAI (link)
・Google Gemini (link)
・Google Cloud (Vertex AI) (link)
When processed via our servers, all requests are sent to Google Cloud (Vertex AI) endpoints. Whichever model is used on Vertex AI (Google or partner models), Vertex AI (Google Cloud) privacy and terms apply; your prompts and responses are not shared with third parties (including model providers).
We may switch the AI service used without notice for improved functionality.

(5) Data and AI Training
When we use Google Cloud (Vertex AI) via our servers, Google Cloud's Service Specific Terms (Section 17, "Training Restriction") contractually guarantee that data we send will not be used for training or fine-tuning Google's AI/ML models without our prior permission. This restriction applies to all managed models on Vertex AI (including partner models). When other AI providers are used, their respective terms apply.

(6) Data Handling

  • Images and PDFs are deleted from our server immediately after analysis when processed via our server. Data sent to each AI provider is processed in accordance with that provider's privacy policy.
  • When we use Google Cloud (Vertex AI) via our servers, we follow Google Cloud's privacy policy and Cloud Data Processing Addendum.

(7) Protection of Personal Information
We strive not to extract personally identifiable information (name, address, phone number, etc.) that may appear in images. We cannot guarantee complete prevention given the nature of image content.

(8) Push Notifications (Google Firebase)
We use Firebase from Google LLC to send push notifications when analysis is complete.
・Information stored: device identifier, analysis completion records
・Google privacy (link)
・If you disable notifications, we do not register new identifiers.

(9) Data Transfer (Android)
To transfer your charge balance when changing devices, we may offer sign-in with Google or other Android-specific methods. See in-app settings for details.

(10) Advertising
This app does not display ads.

3. Purpose of Use

Collected information is used only for: extracting schedule information from images or PDFs, registering extracted schedules to the calendar, improving app functionality and fixing defects.

This app does NOT use information for: user behavior tracking, selling or providing data to third parties (except AI analysis use).

4. Data Retention

  • Image and PDF data: Deleted immediately after analysis (retention: seconds)
  • Extracted schedule information: Deleted from the app after calendar registration
  • Registered calendar events: Stored in the OS default calendar app (until the user deletes them)

This app does not store images or schedule content on servers. We may store or retain the following: free-tier usage (usage count and month linked to a device identifier); push notification identifiers on Google's service; usage and purchase-related records in our systems for a certain period for fraud prevention and service operation.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except: when required by law, when you have given consent, as described in "Third-Party Services" (AI analysis use) above.

6. Security

We implement the following to protect your information: temporary processing of images only (no permanent storage), encrypted communication, minimization of in-app data, and when we use Google Cloud (Vertex AI) via our servers, contract terms prohibiting use of data for AI training.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to: revoke access to camera, photos, calendar, and notifications (via Android settings), delete the app (all app data will be removed), delete calendar events (via the OS default calendar app).

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. If a child under 13 uses this app, it should be with parental consent and supervision.

This Privacy Policy may be changed without notice due to legal changes or app updates. Significant changes will be notified within the app.
